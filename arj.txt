Article
Artificial intelligence is becoming increasingly important in education. AI can help provide personalized learning experiences by adapting educational content to individual students. It can also support teachers by assisting with tasks such as grading, lesson planning, and providing feedback. AI-based tools can make learning more interactive and accessible for students. However, the use of AI in education also creates challenges. These include concerns about data privacy, unequal access to technology, inaccurate information, and excessive dependence on automated systems. Therefore, educational institutions need to use AI responsibly while ensuring that teachers and students remain actively involved in the learning process. Indian Journal of Educational Technology
